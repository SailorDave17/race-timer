# Mad Cow Race Timer

A start-sequence timer for sailboat racing. It runs standalone on a Wear OS watch, with no phone
required on the water, and standalone on an Android phone for the committee-boat console.

- [Privacy policy](privacy-policy) -- the app collects no data at all
- [Source code](https://github.com/SailorDave17/race-timer)

This site exists to host the privacy policy at a stable address. It is deliberately not the
project's documentation, which lives in the repository.
